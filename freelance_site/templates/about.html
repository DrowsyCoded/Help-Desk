I'm Jonathon Hunt, a self-taught developer and writer based in Medford, Oregon. I taught myself web development and copywriting outside of a traditional office job, the same way I've taught myself most of what I know: by building things, breaking them, and figuring out why.

Before this became my full-time focus, I spent years in manufacturing and retail. I worked as a machine operator and forklift driver at a local mill, and before that I spent time in retail management and food service. Those are the kinds of jobs where you learn how to talk to people, solve problems on the spot, and get things done without excuses. That background still shapes how I run this business. I show up, I communicate clearly, and I don't disappear in the middle of a project.

I started freelancing in early 2025, teaching myself web design, resume writing, and content strategy while still working full time. Over the past year and a half that work has grown into the real focus of what I do, built one project and one client relationship at a time, not through an agency or a team.

There's no agency here. No account managers, no outsourcing, no handoffs. When you work with me, you're working directly with the person designing your site or writing your content, which means fewer people in the loop and someone who actually understands what you asked for.

I build with AI-assisted tools, and I'm upfront about that from the start. The tools help me move faster. They don't replace judgment. Everything I deliver gets reviewed, tested, and refined by hand before it ever reaches you.

That's the whole approach: build things properly, tell you the truth about how they're built, and be someone you can actually reach when you need to.
